## About the reels

The view program drew one frame for every chosen step of the trajectory integration and photographed each on a CRT; MSC cut sequences of them into the film *View from a Spacecraft*. These reels are computed the same way, a display list per step, from the recreation's trajectory and attitude:

- **Earth, translunar coast** — Figure 6's framing, every 3 minutes for five hours.
- **Moon on the approach** — Figure 7's framing, every 15 minutes, until the Moon fills the field.
- **Scanning telescope, rev 30** — Figure 3a's attitude held for two hours: the stars stand still and the Moon's horizon sweeps through as the command module orbits.
- **LM, turning** — the hidden-line lunar module, 3° a frame.

Nothing plays until you press Play. "Film grain" lays a light grain over the frame; it is decoration, not data.

## Limits

These are the recreation's frames, not scans of the 1969 film, and they share the recreation's recorded differences from the originals (the horizon rule, the hatching).
